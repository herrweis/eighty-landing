The name

eighty° is written lowercase with the degree symbol attached, capital only when it opens a sentence. Always written as eighty°, never spelled out as Eighty Degrees Recovery and Wellness Centre.

Typing the °

Mac: press Shift + Option + 8.

Windows: hold Alt and type 0176 on the number pad. On a laptop without one, press Windows + . (full stop) to open the symbol panel and pick ° from the symbols tab.

It is the degree sign. The look-alike º (Option + 0 on a Mac) is a different character and will show up wrong in some fonts.

Size and space

Keep clear space around the wordmark of at least the height of the “e”. Do not set the wordmark smaller than 16px on screen or 12mm in print. Do not stretch, recolour or add effects.

Signage

eighty° signage should feel quiet and integrated into the space. It is there to identify, guide and inform, not to fill every available surface.

The name

  • Use the supplied wordmark artwork.
  • Never retype or recreate the name.
  • The ° is part of the wordmark.
  • Avoid repeating the logo within the same view.

Typography

  • Use iA Writer Mono Regular for room names, wayfinding and functional information.
  • Lowercase, with wide letter spacing. Use the tagline artwork on this page as the visual reference.
  • Keep type small. Smaller type needs a little more spacing, larger type a little less.
  • Left align where practical.

Scale

  • Err on the smaller side.
  • Signage should feel confident without dominating the architecture.
  • Give the wordmark and typography plenty of surrounding space.

Colour and finish

  • Keep applications simple and matte.
  • Use Carbon on light surfaces and Linen on dark surfaces wherever possible.
  • Choose materials that work with the interior rather than introduce a new visual language.

Glass

  • Frosting, vinyl and small typography can all work.
  • Keep information secondary to the overall arrival experience.
  • Avoid covering large areas unless privacy requires it.

Room and wayfinding signs

  • Keep room identification simple and consistent.
  • Applied lettering, vinyl, stencil or acrylic can all work depending on cost and surface.
  • Resolve final placement and sizing against the actual space with the sign maker.

Environmental graphics

  • Use one brand element at a time: wordmark, mark, slogan or image.
  • Avoid combining several into one application.
  • Leave space. Not every wall needs something on it.

Avoid

  • Oversized logos.
  • Repeating the brand unnecessarily.
  • Glossy finishes.
  • Outlines, drop shadows or decorative effects.
  • Adding graphics simply to fill space.
